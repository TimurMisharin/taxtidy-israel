# TaxTidy Israel privacy notice

This notice covers the public OpenAI directory edition of TaxTidy Israel, an open-source skill for organizing Israeli tax documents and checking supporting evidence. Users can start with a tax year and goal or use fictional examples throughout.

## What the skill uses

TaxTidy may use a selected tax year, a general description of the user's situation, and the minimum anonymized or redacted payroll, withholding, donation and other tax figures needed for a requested checklist, reconciliation or illustrative estimate. Before processing a financial excerpt or file, TaxTidy explains what it needs and asks the user to confirm. Case labels should be aliases. Keep government identifiers, payment card data, health records, credentials, complete account numbers, signatures and unredacted originals out of this workflow.

## Who receives information

The public skill has no TaxTidy-operated server or telemetry in the shipped code. Content you send in a ChatGPT or Codex session is handled by OpenAI under its applicable [privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/) and account or organization settings. Optional local tools read and write in the private working environment provided for your case. They do not send case contents to the maintainer or connect to mailboxes, brokers, or tax portals. The maintainer and GitHub may see what you voluntarily post in the public support tracker. Use fictional examples in support posts.

## Retention and controls

The public skill sends no case records to the TaxTidy maintainer for storage. Local case files and working copies remain in the directory you choose until you remove them. You control that directory and can delete its contents when finished. Chat and file retention follow the host's settings and policies. In ChatGPT, you can manage chats and applicable settings through [Data Controls](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-data-controls-faq). Public GitHub posts follow [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

For software or privacy questions about TaxTidy, contact the maintainer through [TaxTidy Israel GitHub Issues](https://github.com/TimurMisharin/taxtidy-israel/issues). Describe the issue without identifying data or attachments from your tax case.
