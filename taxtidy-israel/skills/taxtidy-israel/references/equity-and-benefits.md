# Equity and benefit evidence

Use the user's language and preserve official Hebrew names. This release records and reconciles equity events; it does not enable equity tax arithmetic. Any equity record blocks an ordinary annual estimate under the narrow 2025 pack. Do not omit the equity to obtain a number. Collect independently for other years.

Record grant, vest, purchase, sale, transfer and dividend separately with their actual dates, owner, plan, security, employer, administrator, broker and Israeli trustee (נאמן). Keep December purchase and January sale in their respective years. A vest is not evidence of a sale. A reserve payslip is not evidence of regular military employment.

For an ESPP sale, compare trade confirmation, payroll/Form106 benefit, employer tax calculation, adjusted basis and trustee statement. Link the wage component to the existing employer-year wage item; do not add that component to annual wages again. A separate capital result needs proceeds, adjusted basis, fees/currency and applicable year rules; this numeric branch remains disabled. Ask the trustee responsible for the sale to confirm the payroll amount and any separately reportable capital gain/loss.

A dividend can appear on broker and trustee reports. Compare issuer, account/business reference, plan/security, date, gross amount/currency, tax and destination of net cash. Equal financial fields, even with a known destination, do not establish one payment. Keep the observations and questions separate until a reviewed institution statement links their exact records to its payment reference. Ask the trustee: “Were these account/reference entries the same dividend? Please identify the payment reference, gross currency/amount, withholding, receiving account and net cash movement.”

Record that confirmation as `dividend_match` using the [case format](case-format.md): institution, payment reference and hashes of the exact linked observations, with source text/page/field and manual `event`/`payment_link` review. The institution must be a broker/trustee recorded on each linked event. Do not invent a shared ID or turn a taxpayer's assertion into verified evidence. Projection retains each source/account/reference and shows `match_evidence` only for compatible active observations with known amounts/tax/destination. Check all applicable claims sharing taxpayer/year/institution/payment reference, including disjoint observation sets. Contradictory values or different observation sets for that payment leave every affected question open. Corroborating confirmations must identify the same institution/payment and exact observation set; otherwise obtain a corrected complete confirmation. Explicit source-backed correction must supersede the incompatible proof; additional evidence alone does not silently erase it. Different payments or institutions remain independent unless they claim the same observation.

If corrective evidence replaces an earlier record, use explicit source-backed supersedes links, preserving all originals. Linkage to the old hash does not automatically apply to its replacement: review and record a fresh confirmation of the replacement. A new source cannot silently erase an older dispute. Even a proven duplicate dividend does not enable the unsupported annual equity calculation.

For a broker migration record old/new accounts and reconcile holdings, transfers, sales and cash. A transfer without both account identities generates a broker inquiry. Do not infer a sale or loss from a transfer or portfolio decline. Ask for טופס 867 (annual bank/broker tax certificate), trustee event report and Form1042-S where relevant; explain that each establishes different income/withholding or basis evidence. Foreign facts block the current annual calculator.

For reserves, reconcile טופס 106 with the annual National Insurance certificate and the benefit/assistance decision. The phase-2 supported direct employee reserve path requires same-year event/settlement dates and separate withholding evidence. An unexplained assistance payment supports only the documented conditional scenarios; ask the payer for the exact program, award decision, annual certificate and whether payroll already included it. Never classify from a payment label or lack of withholding alone. Keep the user's explanation separate from official evidence.
