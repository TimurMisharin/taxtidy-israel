# Reporting routes and portal evidence

Choose the route for each taxpayer and tax year. Record the source, year and reason for the decision. The utility stores reviewed decisions and evidence in the private case. It supplies no portal, employer or email connection of its own.

## Current year and payroll

- Start with [עריכת תיאום מס באופן מקוון](https://www.gov.il/he/service/tax-coordination-online), public control **לעריכת תיאום מס**. Check employee relief eligibility even for one employer. In **האזור האישי**, check the latest certificate, saved draft and letters before starting a new request. Verify whether an official extension applies to this taxpayer, payer and period, or whether the Authority has issued or announced an update. Use the existing coordination update process when eligible.
- For each payer, collect the latest **תלוש שכר**, taxable pay and withholding year to date, expected remaining taxable pay, separately identifiable bonuses and relief already applied. Read cumulative values once per payer. Do not add monthly pay already included in them. For a closed year, reconcile the monthly slips against **טופס 106** using the same pay and withholding fields and covered period. Compare the latest slip and cumulative amounts for locality, reserve-duty, family and donation relief. Verify the relevant year's rules and explain gaps before estimating. The forecast records these inputs and produces gross income only. The local annual calculation remains limited to the supported 2025 profile.
- Record eligibility, certificate issuance (including a verified extension or a replacement), and observed application separately with `payroll_relief`. Use the exact intended payer and certificate/request version. After issuance, identify the payer for delivery and check the next available payslip and cumulative withholding. A payslip demonstrating relief can also establish application without a certificate for integrated payroll. Review any discrepancy with that payer before asking for relief again.
- Record an online blocker with the exact message and affected payer/step. The service page lists online exclusions and directs excluded taxpayers to the appropriate **משרד פקיד השומה** by appointment. For access or technical failures, use the official support route listed on the service page and verify the appropriate alternative for that case. Keep authentication and one-time verification inside the official interface.

## Completed years

1. Check existing drafts, transmissions, acknowledgments and assessments for the exact taxpayer/year/form/version. Resolve a pending acknowledgment before retrying. If a return already exists, verify the correction procedure for that return and assessment with the responsible **פקיד השומה**. For a genuine omission, track receipt of supporting evidence and issuance of an amended **הודעת שומה** as separate events.
2. For a new filing, establish mandatory filing independently of the estimated result. Verify the year's **טופס 135** exclusions and **טופס 1301** requirements. Review self-employment, foreign income/assets, securities, rental income, retirement, residence/family changes, representation, and prior filings. Check the schedules for the activity and year, including **טפסים 1322, 1324, 1325 ו-134** where applicable.
3. The [טופס 135 refund service](https://www.gov.il/he/service/itc135) has the public control **להגשת הבקשה** for eligible individuals outside mandatory annual filing. Verify the refund window separately from the mandatory filing deadline. For example, the published refund deadline for 2020 is 31 December 2026. If the online refund route is unavailable and eligibility is established, the service lists **מערכת פניות לציבור** and physical delivery to income-tax offices as alternatives. A refund-only file that blocks the correct return needs an official answer about the file classification and form.
4. Use the matching year's **טופס 1301** guidance. The [2025 example](https://www.gov.il/he/service/reporting-and-payment-2025-annual-tax-report-for-individuals) describes full online filing under **הזנת דו''ח שנתי מלא ליחיד**, and combined transmission and signed delivery under **הזנת דו''ח שנתי ליחיד**. Check available channels and eligibility for combined or manual filing for that year before using them. Keep the refund-claim and mandatory-return deadlines in separate `deadline` records. Preserve the base deadline and record any verified applicable extension and source. Continue independent years while a deadline or route remains unresolved.

## Spouses and family returns

For each tax year, establish marital status and its effective date, the **בן הזוג הרשום** (registered spouse), the family file and its classification, both spouses' returns and assessments on record, and any written instructions from **פקיד השומה** (assessing office). Check the exact year, return type and filing channel against official instructions. Preserve the private evidence and the return/version references. If the form, records or written instructions conflict, ask the responsible office which route applies for the existing family file and leave the route unresolved while awaiting confirmation. Access each spouse's records only within that spouse's authorization, using separate private cases where needed.

- **Joint return** (דוח משותף) reports both spouses' income. Section 65 generally attributes spouses' income to the registered spouse. Section 66 permits **separate tax calculation** (חישוב מס נפרד) for qualifying income and sets conditions for shared sources and other income. Review those conditions before recording a separate calculation within the joint return.
- **Separate spouse submissions** (הגשת דוחות נפרדים) may be available. Under section 131(a)(1) the registered spouse may omit the other spouse's income when the other spouse has filed a separate return or a signed declaration of separate reporting is attached. Section 131(a)(2) addresses the declaring spouse's filing obligation. The 2025 annual guide explains that the assessing office may receive the two submissions as one joint report. A declaration or checkbox records an intention or form selection. Verify the case-specific route and how to link each report to the family file before recommending it. For a paper Form 135 refund claim the public service instructs spouses filing together to report both incomes on the same form. Check the instructions for the exact filing channel and year before applying any other treatment.
- **Form 4430** (טופס 4430) records how to file for the status-change year covered by the applicable instructions, such as the year of marriage or return from separated status. Check the election recorded by the Authority for that year, including the official online update where applicable. For the separate option, review the supporting declarations/signatures required by the year's instructions. A signed copy or a draft portal selection cannot establish that the election was recorded or that the route remains available. If a return or assessment already exists, obtain written confirmation that the chosen route is still available before recommending a separate submission. Reverify later years independently.
- **Correction or amendment** (תיקון דוח) is the route when the registered spouse already filed and the office directs joint reporting. Obtain the exact filed version, acknowledgment, attachments and latest assessment. Preserve the original and prepare the correction separately. Reconcile both spouses' income and credits, map joint assets and withholding to their source records, and use the account opening/joining date, transfers and entitlement to prevent duplicate entries. Follow the written direction and the applicable technical or substantive correction procedure. Track the received attachment, acceptance of the correction, and the revised **הודעת שומה** (assessment notice) as distinct observations. A received attachment proves delivery only. Verify any applicable deadlines or assessment-stage requirements separately.

Official starting points for review:

- [Income Tax Ordinance, sections 65–66 and 131(a)(1)–(2)](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/law_pkudat_mas_hachnasa) and [Knesset legislation record](https://main.knesset.gov.il/Activity/Legislation/Laws/pages/lawprimary.aspx?lawitemid=2000944).
- [2025 annual return guide](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/income-tax-guide-knowyourright/he/Guides_IncomeTax_da-2025.pdf), including the spouse and status-change instructions, and the official [2025 Form 1301](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/service/reporting-and-payment-2025-annual-tax-report-for-individuals/he/Service_Pages_Income_tax_annual-report-2026_1301-2025-ACC.pdf), [2025 Form 135](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/service/reporting-and-payment-2025-annual-tax-report-for-individuals/he/Service_Pages_Income_tax_annual-report-2026_itc135-2025.pdf) and [year-specific Form 135 downloads and filing instructions](https://www.gov.il/he/service/itc135).
- [Form 4430 and its notes](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/service/family-status-change-annual-report-4430/he/itc4430.pdf), [status-change service](https://www.gov.il/he/service/family-status-change-annual-report-4430) and [Instruction 13/2021 for online refund files](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/inst-13-2021/he/IncomeTax_inst-13-2021.pdf).
- [Instruction 3/2023, section 8.4.4](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/inst-03-2023/he/IncomeTax_inst-03-2023.pdf) and [Instruction 3/2018 on correction requests](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/income-tax-inst-03-2018/he/Policy_IncomeTaxInst_hor_3-2018_acc.pdf).

Use `route_status: unresolved` when the year, applicable form/channel or case evidence does not establish a route. Explain the missing evidence, such as a recorded 4430 election, proof of the existing report, or written directions confirming how the office will handle separate submissions or a correction. Keep known deadlines and collection work visible while the route is unresolved. The local validator checks reviewed evidence and consistency. The assessor decides how the case is processed.

## Section 46 and Israel Donations

- Use the [Section 46 approval lookup](https://www.gov.il/he/service/confirmation-of-donations) and check approval on the relevant date. Match original receipts and payment/settlement evidence by issuer plus receipt ID for the taxpayer and year. Check donor, amount, approvals and dates. Keep the eligible donation amount and the resulting tax credit separate. For cross-year installments, verify the specific payment method and applicable rule before assigning a year. The [issuer reporting service](https://www.gov.il/he/service/report-public-institution-receipt-donation) has a specific rule for postdated checks, with receipts by tax year based on the checks' settlement dates.
- From 1 January 2026, Section 46 institutions must report donations and cancellations through [תרומות ישראל](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/info_digital_donation_system). Compare the individual entries in **האזור האישי**, including cancellations and taxpayer association. Keep an original or emailed receipt as reviewed evidence where supported. Record an absent entry as `missing`, a mismatch as `wrong_taxpayer`, or a verified cancellation as `cancelled`. Use the issuer's verified contact to draft a request naming the specific receipt and asking for reporting or correction and confirmation. Check for an existing request. Send only with explicit authorization for the recipient and purpose. Recheck the individual registry entry before recording `associated`.
- Verify which relief route applies. For in-year **תיאום מס**, review the relevant digital entries and the resulting certificate for the intended payer. For integrated payroll, check that the primary employer's payroll software is connected and obtain explicit taxpayer authorization for that employer's access through **האזור האישי**. Under [הוראת ביצוע 12/2025](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/inst-12-2025/he/IncomeTax_inst-12-2025.pdf), the employer receives an eligible cumulative donation amount, while a coordination certificate conveys the credit amount. Check payroll for overlap before requesting another credit. After year-end, verify the applicable **טופס 135**, **טופס 1301**, or correction route. Where registry data are unavailable or disputed, verify the Authority's permitted evidence route for that taxpayer and year.
- Reconcile the actual credit applied in payroll before seeking relief. The existing annual estimate uses actual withholding and calculates the lawful annual credit once. Reconcile the eligible donation amount separately from the credit already reflected in payroll, and avoid requesting that applied portion again. Record any source-backed changes affecting tax eligibility with the existing evidence import so conflicts can block an estimate.
- Before proposing a correction, obtain the exact acknowledged filed version, its attachments, its donation field and the breakdown used to reach that total. Map the receipt to the breakdown before recording `included` or `omitted` with `filed_donation`. A newly found email can corroborate an amount already included. An accepted attachment or equal grand total alone cannot establish inclusion. Leave conflicting findings unresolved and verify any genuine omission's official correction route.

## Record and resume

Use `record-event` for observed case opening, draft, validation, transmission, attachment attempts, filing acknowledgment, cancellation, assessment and settlement. Match taxpayer, year, form and version. Store the exact Hebrew portal or assessor message, observation date and official acknowledgment reference in the private case. Save each accepted and rejected attachment attempt with exact filename, hash and purpose. A matching verified acknowledgment establishes completed filing for that version. Keep cancelled versions and their earlier acknowledgments in history and verify any replacement by its own acknowledgment.

When validation or an assessor's instruction conflicts with another source, preserve both, verify the affected year/version and confirm the official next step. Treat refund-checkbox instructions and chronological refiling as case-specific unless official guidance establishes wider scope. Cancel or refile only the identified, authorized years. Before a real submission or change, verify taxpayer, year, form/version, reviewed amounts, attachments, signatures, and authorization. Use available authorized capabilities and authenticate only in the official interface.

For persistent attachment warnings, identify the rejected filename/attempt, confirm the portal policy, correct one verified copy and inspect its observed status. Keep the accepted copy. See the [PDF workflow](pdf-workflow.md). For a blocked route, identify the exact blocker and verify the official alternative. Check any existing inquiry before drafting a new one. On resuming, reopen the exact draft and confirm accepted attachments and recent acknowledgments before attempting another action.
