## Per-year decision

Keep טופס 135 and טופס 1301 as candidates until year-specific review establishes the route and filing obligation. Check self-employment, foreign income/assets, securities, rental income, retirement, residence/family changes, prior filings and representation. Neither employment, a security holding nor a refund/due amount decides eligibility by itself. Tax due must be labeled due and reviewed against filing obligations independently. Relevant supporting schedules may include טפסים 1322, 1324, 1325 and 134; their applicability needs the actual activity/year instructions. No new schedule rules are enabled here.

For spouses collect registered spouse, marriage year, separate/joint incomes, ownership of donations/assets/accounts, account-join date, declarations, signatures and the other return. Separate calculations do not establish separate returns. [Form4430](https://www.gov.il/he/service/family-status-change-annual-report-4430) could not be retrieved; [annual-report index](https://www.gov.il/he/departments/topics/annual-tax-reports-for-itc) had no readable content. Year-specific 4430 rules and mandatory deadlines remain unresolved. Ask the tax office/professional the precise ownership/return question with supported financial impact. Never generate another person's signature.

## Record what was observed

`record-event` stores reviewed source evidence; it does not operate a portal. Match taxpayer, year, form, draft version and exact filename/hash/purpose. Retain each rejected and accepted attempt. Acceptance of a corrected attachment within a draft remains a draft. Filing completed requires a matched acknowledgment; a user statement or transmission alone is insufficient. Summary lists all return versions and keeps any unacknowledged draft visible.

For persistent security warnings inspect which filename/attempt was rejected, consult the portal's upload policy, correct one verified copy and confirm the status beside that exact filename. Keep an accepted copy. Reopen the draft and check the warning. Do not bypass scanning; remove an obsolete rejected entry only if necessary and authorized. Local PDF conversion and reviewed attachment readiness are available through the [PDF workflow](pdf-workflow.md). Local readiness is separate from observed portal acceptance.

A refund-only file blocked for a tax-due return requires the exact portal message and a precise inquiry about proper file classification/form. Check an existing inquiry first. Draft by default; sending needs explicit recipient/purpose authorization and an observed send. Reconnection requires rereading the draft version and accepted attachments before retrying. No utility action transmits or signs a return.

Store base deadline, category, official source and observed date. Apply only a source-backed taxpayer-specific extension explicitly verified as applicable; preserve the base. Corrections use supersedes links. Sort years by earliest effective deadline, keep blocked years visible, and continue independent years. Current-year forecasts show per-payer actual pay/withholding, remaining pay, bonus and relief assumptions as gross income only; no 2025 tax fallback is permitted for 2026.
