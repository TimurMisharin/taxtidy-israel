## Exact supported profile

Full-year adult male Israeli resident under 60, unmarried, complete declared income and payments, no pension/insurance contributions, other entitlements/deductions, foreign facts or prior loss/donation carry-ins. This is a deliberately small subset of taxpayers; most ordinary payroll cases have additional facts and need later functionality. The reviewed intake must establish each coverage answer. Missing is not zero. All source amounts/withholding must be known ILS, nonnegative, with at most two decimal digits. No other numeric year is enabled.

Securities must be sole-owned domestic listed shares, nonbusiness/nonrelated-party and nonmaterial holdings, at the verified 25% rate. Explicitly exclude interest, dividends, foreign tax/currency and carry-in losses. A portfolio decline is not a realized loss. Gross proceeds remain separate from taxable gains. Loss does not itself equal a refund: liability is compared with actual withholding and distinct advance payments. If this profile has only ILS 3,000 net gain, ILS 750 capital tax is absorbed by available resident credits; a bank's ILS 2,000 withholding therefore yields an estimated refund of ILS 2,000 for 2025.

Donations require owner/receipt/date-specific Section 46 approval and settled same-year payment evidence. No allocation inference from monthly credit-card charges. Installments, fees, benefits, cancellations, uncertain settlement and carry-ins block this scope. Record a payroll credit mismatch as a reconciliation question; actual withheld tax already incorporates payroll relief.

Ordinary taxable income is reconciled wages plus supported direct reserve payments plus any explicitly taxable assistance scenario. Add capital liability, apply available personal credit, then the allowed donation credit. Subtract actual Israeli withholding and distinct advances. Positive signed balance is estimated additional tax; negative is estimated refund. Foreign tax and allowable foreign credit are separate zero fields only because any foreign facts block this scope. Deductions are zero only under the complete reviewed profile.

The estimate preserves exact decimal arithmetic. The guide directs whole-ILS form fields but the statutory stage/direction of fractional rounding was not established. `filing_ready` is always false. The public official wage simulator was exercised with synthetic 2025 annual wages ILS 120,000 and 2.25 points: displayed tax ILS 6,901 versus exact ILS 6,901.20, a difference of ILS 0.20. This checks only that wage/credit scenario, not full annual return, donation, securities or reserve outcomes.

## Assistance inquiry and independent years

For an unexplained ILS 2,000 assistance entry, do not infer taxability from employment, its label or zero withholding. Draft to the payer: “Please identify the exact assistance program and legal payment category, whether this replaces employee income or is a Section 18 special/additional reserve grant, and provide the award decision and annual income/withholding certificate. Is this amount already included in the employer's טופס 106 (Form 106)?” In the synthetic example, conditional 2025 liability is ILS 8,858 excluded or ILS 9,258 taxable, a difference of ILS 400; both are scenarios, with no final classification established. Send only with explicit recipient/purpose authorization. Continue collection for other years.
